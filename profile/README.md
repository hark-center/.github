# Hark Center

**Better conversations. Deeper insights. Meaningful impact.**

Hark is a collaborative intelligence platform designed to help organizations capture, understand, and act on the conversations that matter.

From conferences and workshops to facilitated discussions and breakout sessions, Hark transforms conversations into actionable insights—helping teams uncover shared themes, identify emerging ideas, and make informed decisions.

## What We're Building

We believe valuable insights shouldn't disappear when a conversation ends.

Hark brings together real-time transcription, AI-powered analysis, and collaborative workflows to make it easier to turn discussions into meaningful outcomes.

Our platform is designed around four core capabilities:

- **Capture** — Record and transcribe conversations across sessions and breakout groups.
- **Organize** — Bring events, sessions, facilitators, and recordings together in one place.
- **Understand** — Discover themes, perspectives, and insights across conversations.
- **Act** — Turn collective knowledge into reports, decisions, and next steps.

## Our Mission

To make every conversation count.

We're building tools that help people listen more effectively, understand diverse perspectives, and unlock the collective intelligence of their communities.

## Built for Meaningful Conversations

Hark supports organizations bringing people together to share ideas, solve problems, and shape what's next.

Whether it's a small facilitated workshop or a large multi-session conference, our goal is simple: make it easier to capture what matters and turn conversations into progress.
